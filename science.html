What happens inside the skin.

Four physiological mechanisms behind the glow. Tap a card to see how each one works.

How: 630nm photons are absorbed in the skin, switching on fibroblasts. More active fibroblasts produce collagen and elastin, the proteins behind firm, bouncy-looking skin.
How: Light is absorbed by an enzyme in the mitochondria (cytochrome c oxidase), which helps cells make more ATP. Energized cells carry out repair and renewal more efficiently.
How: Low-level EMS pulses mimic the body’s bioelectric signals so facial muscles contract and release, like a light workout that improves tone and contour.
How: LED warmth and muscle activation widen tiny capillaries, delivering more oxygen and nutrients and helping clear metabolic waste, which gives a fresher, more radiant look.
