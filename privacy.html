Section 1

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

NIKORA respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, track a parcel, start a return, write a review or contact us, we collect the personal data needed to help you:

  • Identity and contact details: your name, email address and phone number (if you provide one).
  • Delivery details: your shipping address and order number.
  • Support and returns: the messages, return reasons and photos you choose to send us.
  • Reviews: your display name, rating, review text and optional photo, which are published on our site.
  • Cart contents: saved in your browser and kept in sync under a random cart ID that is not linked to your name.

Your order and support data is processed securely through Firebase, Google’s cloud database platform, so that we can fulfil your order.

How We Use Your Data

  • Processing, packing and delivering your orders.
  • Sending order confirmations and tracking updates.
  • Providing customer support at nikora.support@gmail.com.
  • Handling returns, refunds and loss/damage claims.
  • Keeping our website secure and meeting our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We share only what is necessary with trusted providers who help us run the store:

  • Secure payment gateways process your payment at checkout. Card details are entered only on the checkout page. NIKORA never asks for card numbers by email, chat or contact form.
  • Carrier logistics partners receive your name, delivery address and contact details so they can deliver your parcel and provide tracking.
  • Firebase (Google) provides the secure database infrastructure for orders, returns, reviews and support messages, and data travels over encrypted connections.

Your GDPR / CCPA Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your personal data, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format, and object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a request, email nikora.support@gmail.com from the address you used with us and include your order number if you have one. We may verify your identity first and will respond within the time required by applicable law. We will never treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

Shipping, Returns & Guarantee Policy

Fast delivery, a risk-free trial and a simple return process, so you can try NIKORA with confidence.

Delivery Timelines

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for change-of-mind returns. Returns apply to unopened/unused items within 30 days. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of your return being checked in.

Cookies & Security

We keep tracking to a minimum. NIKORA uses only what is needed to make shopping work.

Essential Cookies & Browser Storage

We use essential session cookies and browser storage only. They are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

  • Shopping cart: keeps your items and any promo code as you move between pages.
  • Theme preference: remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • Form safeguards: a short timer that prevents duplicate submissions.
  • Beauty Concierge chat: your conversation stays in your browser tab until you close it.

You can clear this data any time in your browser settings. Your cart and preferences will simply reset.

Security & Retention

Your information is stored on Firebase’s secure infrastructure and sent over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password for your email account and never share payment details by chat or email.

We keep order and support records only as long as needed to fulfil your order, honor the 30-day guarantee and loss/damage protection claims, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Exercising Your Data Rights

To access, correct or delete your personal data, or to ask us to remove a review, email nikora.support@gmail.com. We’re happy to help.
